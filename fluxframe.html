Keyboard-navigable, reduced-motion aware

A surface that reacts, not just renders.

Every KPI card has a cursor spotlight, an animated counter and a sparkline that draws itself on entry. Numbers occasionally tick with a soft pulse — a subtle signal that the data is alive.

AI insight that streams.

The forecast panel doesn't dump a paragraph — it types itself out with a blinking caret. Chips below swap the topic mid-stream, framing the assistant as a colleague thinking on its feet.

Command palette as the spine.

⌘K opens a Linear-style overlay for search, navigation and quick actions. Power users never leave the keyboard; casual users still discover it via the top-bar hint.

Every pixel is a control.

The chart shows tooltips at data points, the donut isolates on click, sidebar nav swaps the page, notifications and profile menus open in place, the filter tabs actually filter — nothing here is a placeholder.
